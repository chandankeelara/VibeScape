        P1 · Signature — design notes

        Spotify's frame, Apple's hand, and one instrument that lights the room.

        1. The frame is Spotify's, untouched. Search in the middle of the top bar, the stage, the queue rail, a persistent bar with progress under the transport and times on either side. Phone: everything on one screen, the queue in a sheet behind an "Up next" bar, Audio | Video at the top where YouTube Music puts it.
        2. The finish is Apple's. Inter at tight optical tracking, bare transport glyphs with hover discs, one solid disc for play. Pause eases the cover back to 90% with the file's one spring and dims the light under it.
        3. Depth is light, not shadow. On near-black a drop shadow is invisible, so the cover lights the floor in its own colour, panels sit one value step up with a 1px top rim, and the bar is vibrancy that the stage really scrolls under. Light theme swaps to real shadows.
        4. The vibe is the jewel. The five-band spectrum is inlaid in a groove and lit up to a pearl, so the colour under the pearl is exactly the accent everywhere else. An accent stone and a halo sit on the pearl; four notches mark the bands and the drag settles onto them. It is a real range input: arrows, PgUp/PgDn by band, click anywhere, screen-reader values.
        5. The accent is solved, not picked. Hue walks cool to warm; the text tone is stepped until it clears 4.6:1 on every surface, badge and tinted room it can land on, in both themes, at every value 0–100. Dragging re-lights instantly; clicking a band dissolves over 360ms.
        6. Rows scan like Spotify, quieter. Art, two lines, then a trailing column of facts: each track's vibe as a pip in its own band colour beside the number, and its language. Actions reveal on hover and are always there on touch.
        7. Micro-interactions are tactile, then still. A press that gives (0.92–0.98), a seek line that thickens and shows where you would land, a cover that crossfades with a 3% settle, a segmented lozenge that slides. 140–520ms, nothing loops except the tiny equalizer.
        8. One element, every layout. The video surface is always in the DOM; audio, video, theater and phone only restyle it. The YouTube iframe would mount once and never move.
        9. Bit speaks for the DJ. One quiet line under the vibe ("Because you played…", "Steering away after a skip"); he reacts once and rests. Hide him from the account menu.
