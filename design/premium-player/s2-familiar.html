            Your vibe

            61hype
            Mini player

            S2 · Familiar — design notes

            The frame people already know from Spotify and YouTube Music, with one new idea: the vibe.

            1. Borrowed structure, on purpose. Top bar with search in the middle, now-playing panel, queue on the right, a persistent player bar along the bottom. On phone: a mini-player that expands, a queue sheet. Nobody learns anything.
            2. Progress above transport, YouTube Music style. The seek line runs along the top edge of the bar — neutral at rest, accent with a thumb on hover. Elapsed and total flank the transport so play stays dead centre.
            3. The vibe is the one signature. A real range input (keyboard, screen reader and click-to-set for free) with detent ticks at the band edges and five tappable band names. A compact chip in the bar opens the same control. Dragging re-tints the accent live.
            4. The accent is computed, not picked. Hue walks cool → warm (sleep blue, chill cyan, steady violet, hype pink, beast orange). Text and fill tones are solved per value so every accent text clears 4.6:1 on every surface, both themes.
            5. Depth by value, not shadow. In dark, panels step bg → panel → raised → hover; the cover throws a soft light pool in its own colour rather than a black drop shadow. Light theme uses real shadows.
            6. The art tint is static. A low-lightness gradient from the cover colour, crossfaded on track change. It is generated at a fixed lightness so the brightest point still keeps grey text above AA.
            7. One element, every layout. The phone now-playing screen IS the desktop panel restyled; the bar IS the mini-player. The video surface is always in the DOM and only changes visibility, so the YouTube iframe mounts once and never moves.
            8. Bit speaks for the DJ. He sits beside the one quiet line that explains the pick ("Because you played…", "Steering away after a skip"), reacts once and returns to rest. Hide him from the account menu.
            9. Motion is 150–300 ms, then still. Cover crossfade, sheet slides, a play/pause glyph morph. The equalizer eases down on pause rather than freezing. Reduced motion keeps readable resting poses.
            10. Not on phone: picture-in-picture. Document PiP does not exist in mobile browsers, so the control is not rendered there rather than shown disabled.
