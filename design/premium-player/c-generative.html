C — Generative / Club

The vibe is the light. One number drives everything you see.
  1. One function of vibe. Palette, field speed, particle density, streak length, tempo of the pulse and the weight of the type all come from the same 0–100 value, eased per frame so drags feel physical.
  2. Type weight is energy. Unbounded is variable 200→900. The vibe number, mood word and background word get heavier as you push towards beast; track titles take the weight of their own measured vibe.
  3. The fader is its own legend. The track is a waveform that goes from a slow, low swell at 0 to a dense, tall signal at 100. Left of the thumb is lit. It is still a plain range input underneath, so keys and screen readers just work.
  4. Bit rides the fader. The mascot sits on the thumb: dozes in sleep, bobs to the beat, bounces when a song finishes, sulks on a skip, scans while the DJ thinks.
  5. Turntable spin-down. Pause doesn't freeze the field; it decelerates like a deck losing power. Play spins it back up.
  6. Cheap blur. The gradient mesh renders at 1/8 resolution and the browser upscales it, so it costs nothing. There's no backdrop-filter over the moving field and no live SVG noise; the grain is a 128px tile baked once.
  7. The hero never moves in the DOM. Audio, video and theater are grid changes on one element, so a real YouTube iframe would never re-mount.
  8. The DJ explains itself. The eyebrow says why a track is playing, e.g. "near Peach in embedding space" or "steering away after a skip".
